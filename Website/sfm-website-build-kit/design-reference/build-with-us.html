What it asks of you.

You do not need to live in any particular place. Most of our work happens online, and the teams are coordinated on our own internal tool. We welcome everyone, but we keep high standards, so you may be invited to a short interview before you join the team. There are no fixed hours: the work is organised by project, and you make your own time. The one firm commitment is Sunday, when core team members work all day.
