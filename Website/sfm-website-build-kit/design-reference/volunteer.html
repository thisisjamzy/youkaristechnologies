Volunteer

Someone has to bring the strangers together. Why not you?

Every stranger who prays stands on something built by a volunteer: an app that works, a welcome that feels warm, a message that reached them at the right moment, a prayer guide they can trust. Strangers for Mary is built and carried by people who give their gifts to Mary's mission.

Why volunteer

Your gifts, carried to strangers you will never meet.

The work you do reaches people who will never know your name. A stranger on the other side of the world prays their Rosary on Youkaris, reads a reflection you edited or finds the welcome you wrote, and part of what holds them is yours. That is Cana: carrying to Jesus what we were never asked to carry.

How to volunteer.

We welcome everyone, and we keep high standards. Every role is unpaid and none is full-time, and we train you in the role. Most of the work happens online and is organised by project on our own internal tool, so you make your own time. The one firm commitment is Sunday.
